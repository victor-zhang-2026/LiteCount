## 变更 / Changes

- 事项到达目标时间后自动移入右上角「已过期」，主列表只显示进行中事项。
- 悬浮球角标自动显示进行中事项数；已过期事项仍可编辑或删除。
- 修复新增入口后面板遮挡悬浮球的问题，保持原有界面风格。

- Items move to Expired automatically at their target time; the main list shows upcoming items.
- The floating-ball badge counts upcoming items. Expired items remain editable and deletable.
- Fixed the panel covering the floating ball after the new header button was added.
